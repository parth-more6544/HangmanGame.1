RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

1. OBJECTIVE

Decode the secret hidden word by guessing one letter at a time using your physical keyboard or the onscreen arcade console before six strikes construct the hanging character.

2. THE GALLOWS (6 STRIKES)

You begin with 6 lives. Each incorrect letter draws a new body segment (Head → Torso → Left Arm → Right Arm → Left Leg → Right Leg). On strike 6, the gallows falls and game over is triggered.

3. SCORING FORMULA

  • +10 PTS for each correct letter revealed.
  • +100 PTS bonus for completing the secret word.
  • +15 PTS per remaining attempt/life spared.
  • -20 PTS per hint purchased (score cannot fall below zero).
  • 0 PTS for incorrect or repeated guesses (duplicate guesses never penalize lives).

4. HINT SYSTEM

Hint 1: Reveals a thematic clue about the word.
Hint 2: Automatically unmasks one remaining letter across all its positions without awarding guess points.
